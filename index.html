☀️ SunProof

Track: Renewable Energy & Energy Trading (renewable certificate tracking). Small rooftop solar owners make clean energy but cannot prove it cheaply. SunProof turns hourly meter readings into tamper-evident Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs): audit the data, hash every reading, commit one Merkle root, block double counting, and let anyone verify.

meter readings→audit: rules + learned baseline→SHA-256 Merkle root→1-MWh RECs + registry→public memo / anchor→anyone verifies

Note: readings in this demo are synthetic (clear-sky model + random clouds), not real site data. No backend: all logic runs in your browser.

Yellow = eligible readings, red = excluded by the audit, dashed line = panel capacity.
